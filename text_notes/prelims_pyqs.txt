# UPSC Prelims Physical Geography: 15-Year PYQ Bank & Trend Analysis (2011–2025)
=======================================================

Categorized by Topic • Authentic UPSC Answer Keys • Trap Warnings • High-Yield Predictive MCQs

[OVERVIEW]

  
### 📊 Strategic Macro Analysis: Physical Geography in Prelims

  Physical Geography constitutes between 6 to 12 questions annually in the Civil Services Preliminary Examination (Paper-I). Over the past 15 years, UPSC's questioning philosophy has undergone three distinct evolutionary phases:

  

    Phase 1 (2011–2016) — Conceptual Recall & 1-2-3 Options: Questions heavily emphasized fundamentals from NCERT Class XI (e.g., Coriolis force, rock types, ionospheric reflection, solar constant). Standard elimination techniques worked reliably.

    * Phase 2 (2017–2021) — Multi-Statement Integrated Application: Focus shifted toward cause-and-effect reasoning (e.g., dew formation on clear vs cloudy nights, 21st June solstice daylight, albedo ranking).

    * Phase 3 (2022–2025) — Elimination-Proofing & Statement-I / Statement-II Framing: Introduction of "Only one / Only two / All three / None" pairing and the revival of Assertion-Reasoning (e.g., 2024 question on why the atmosphere is heated more by terrestrial longwave radiation than solar shortwave). Candidates cannot guess through elimination; total conceptual certainty is required.

  

[/OVERVIEW]

## Theme 1: Earth in the Universe, Planetary Motions & Seasons
-------------------------------------------------------

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2021] 
    (Topic: Earth–Sun Relationship & Solstice)

  
  
    On 21st June, the Sun:
  
  

    * (a) does not set below the horizon at the Arctic Circle

    * (b) does not set below the horizon at Antarctic Circle

    * (c) shines vertically overhead at noon on the Equator

    * (d) shines vertically overhead at the Tropic of Capricorn

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Detailed Explanation: On June 21 (Summer Solstice in the Northern Hemisphere), the Northern Hemisphere is tilted towards the Sun. The Sun's rays fall directly overhead at the Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N). Consequently, areas north of the Arctic Circle (66.5°N) experience continuous 24-hour daylight where the Sun does not dip below the horizon (the phenomenon of the Midnight Sun).

      
[TRAP ALERT]

        ⚠️ UPSC Trap Alert: Option (c) is the equinox condition (March 21 & Sept 23), and option (d) describes the December 21/22 Winter Solstice. UPSC deliberately checks if you confuse the Tropic of Cancer with the Tropic of Capricorn or the Equator.
      
[/TRAP ALERT]

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2019] 
    (Topic: Insolation & Solar Noon Position)

  
  
    On 21st June, which of the following locations experiences the longest day length?
  
  

    * (a) Hyderabad

    * (b) Chennai

    * (c) Bhopal

    * (d) Delhi

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (d)]

      Detailed Explanation: During the June Solstice, the Northern Hemisphere is tilted toward the Sun. A foundational rule of physical climatology states: As you move northward (higher latitude in the summer hemisphere), day length progressively increases. Among the four Indian cities, Delhi has the highest northern latitude (~28.6°N), while Bhopal is at ~23.2°N, Hyderabad at ~17.3°N, and Chennai at ~13.0°N. Therefore, Delhi experiences the longest day length (~13 hours 58 minutes).

      
[TRAP ALERT]

        ⚠️ UPSC Trap Alert: Candidates mistakenly select Bhopal because the Tropic of Cancer passes near Bhopal. However, maximum overhead insolation intensity is at the Tropic of Cancer, but day length continues to increase northward all the way to the North Pole (where it reaches 24 hours).
      
[/TRAP ALERT]

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2022] 
    (Topic: Solar Physics & Space Weather)

  
  
    If a major solar storm (solar flare) reaches the Earth, which of the following are the possible effects on Earth?
    

      * GPS and navigation systems could fail.

      * Tsunamis could occur at equatorial regions.

      * Power grids could be damaged.

      * Intense auroras could occur over much of the Earth.

      * Forest fires could take place over large areas of the planet.

      * Orbits of the satellites could be disturbed.

      * Shortwave radio communication could be interrupted.

    

    Select the correct answer using the code given below:
  
  

    * (a) 1, 2, 4 and 5 only

    * (b) 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 only

    * (c) 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 only

    * (d) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (c)]

      Detailed Explanation: Solar flares and Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) inject high-energy charged particles and electromagnetic radiation into Earth's magnetosphere and ionosphere. This causes geomagnetic storms, inducing electrical surges that damage power grids (Statement 3), disrupt High-Frequency (HF) radio communications (Statement 7), distort satellite orbits due to upper atmospheric expansion/drag (Statement 6), impair GPS satellite signal propagation (Statement 1), and trigger intense auroral displays that migrate toward lower latitudes (Statement 4).

      
[TRAP ALERT]

        ⚠️ UPSC Trap Alert: Tsunamis are caused by underwater seismic, volcanic, or mass wasting events, not space weather (Statement 2 is invalid). Solar flares cannot directly trigger planetary-scale forest fires through the atmosphere (Statement 5 is invalid). Eliminating 2 and 5 instantly isolates option (c).
      
[/TRAP ALERT]

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2013] 
    (Topic: Earth Motions & Day Length Variation)

  
  
    Variations in the length of daytime and nighttime from season to season are due to:
  
  

    * (a) the earth's rotation on its axis

    * (b) the earth's revolution round the sun in an elliptical manner

    * (c) latitudinal position of the place

    * (d) revolution of the earth on a tilted axis

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (d)]

      Detailed Explanation: Rotation on its axis merely produces the alternating cycle of day and night. If the Earth's axis had no tilt (0°), day and night would be exactly 12 hours everywhere on Earth throughout the entire year. It is the constant axial tilt (23.44°) combined with revolution around the Sun that causes changing solar elevation and the variation in daytime and nighttime from season to season.

## Theme 2: Earth's Interior, Seismology & Magnetism
-------------------------------------------------------

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2018] 
    (Topic: Earth's Magnetism & Paleomagnetism)

  
  
    Consider the following statements:
    

      * The Earth's magnetic field has reversed every few hundred thousand years.

      * When the Earth was created more than 4000 million years ago, there was 54% oxygen and no carbon dioxide.

      * When living organisms originated, they did not modify the early atmosphere of the Earth.

    

    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  
  

    * (a) 1 only

    * (b) 2 and 3 only

    * (c) 1 and 3 only

    * (d) 1, 2 and 3

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Detailed Explanation: Paleomagnetic studies of oceanic basalts (Vine-Matthews-Morley hypothesis) confirm that Earth's geomagnetic field reverses polarity periodically (every 200,000 to 300,000 years on average; Statement 1 is correct). Earth's primordial atmosphere was reducing and anoxic, dominated by water vapour, nitrogen, CO₂, methane, and ammonia, with virtually no free oxygen (Statement 2 is completely false). The arrival of photosynthetic cyanobacteria dramatically modified the atmosphere by releasing free oxygen, creating the Great Oxidation Event (Statement 3 is false).

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2013] 
    (Topic: Geomorphic Dynamics & Endogenic Forces)

  
  
    Which of the following phenomena might have influenced the evolution of organisms?
    

      * Continental drift

      * Glacial cycles

    

    Select the correct answer using the code given below:
  
  

    * (a) 1 only

    * (b) 2 only

    * (c) Both 1 and 2

    * (d) Neither 1 nor 2

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (c)]

      Detailed Explanation: Continental drift separated landmasses, creating geographical isolation and allopatric speciation (e.g. marsupials in Australia). Glacial cycles (Pleistocene ice ages) altered sea levels, created land bridges (Beringia), and shifted ecological climatic zones, forcing migration and evolutionary adaptation.

## Theme 3: Petrology, Geomorphic Processes & Plate Tectonics
-------------------------------------------------------

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2014] 
    (Topic: Continental Drift Theory & Evidences)

  
  
    Which of the following is/are cited by the scientists as evidence/evidences for the continued expansion of the universe?
    

      * Detection of microwaves in space

      * Observation of redshift phenomenon in space

      * Movement of asteroids in space

      * Occurrence of supernova explosions in space

    

    Select the correct answer using the code given below:
  
  

    * (a) 1 and 2 only

    * (b) 2 only

    * (c) 1, 3 and 4 only

    * (d) None of the above

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Detailed Explanation: The two decisive empirical pillars supporting the Big Bang and expanding universe are: (1) Discovery of Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation (CMBR) by Penzias & Wilson, and (2) Edwin Hubble's observation of galactic redshift (Doppler shift of light to longer wavelengths). Asteroid movements and supernova explosions are stellar/planetary phenomena, not proof of the metric expansion of spacetime.

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2022] 
    (Topic: Petrology & Rock Characteristics)

  
  
    Consider the following statements regarding rocks:
    

      * Igneous rocks are crystalline in structure and are devoid of fossils.

      * Sedimentary rocks are formed from the deposits of pre-existing rocks or pieces of once-living organism that accumulate on Earth's surface.

      * Metamorphic rocks are formed under the influence of extreme heat and pressure without melting.

    

    Which of the statements given above are correct?
  
  

    * (a) 1 and 2 only

    * (b) 2 and 3 only

    * (c) 1 and 3 only

    * (d) 1, 2 and 3

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (d)]

      Detailed Explanation: Igneous rocks solidify from molten magma/lava; high temperatures destroy organic remains, making them completely devoid of fossils (Statement 1 correct). Sedimentary rocks form via lithification of clastic debris or biogenic deposits (Statement 2 correct). Metamorphism is a solid-state recrystallization process occurring under lithostatic pressure and elevated temperature; if complete melting occurs, it forms magma (leading back to igneous rock), not metamorphic rock (Statement 3 correct).

## Theme 4: Atmosphere, Insolation & Climatology
-------------------------------------------------------

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2024] 
    (Topic: Atmospheric Heating & Greenhouse Dynamics)

  
  
    Consider the following statements:
    

    Statement-I: The atmosphere is heated more by incoming solar radiation than by terrestrial radiation.

    Statement-II: Carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases in the atmosphere are good absorbers of long-wave radiation.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?
  
  

    * (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I

    * (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I

    * (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

    * (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (d)]

      Detailed Explanation: Statement-I is completely incorrect. The atmosphere is largely transparent to incoming shortwave solar radiation (only ~14 units out of 100 are absorbed directly by atmospheric gases). Earth's surface absorbs 51 units of shortwave radiation and re-emits it as longwave infrared terrestrial radiation. Greenhouse gases (CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄) strongly absorb this outgoing longwave radiation, heating the troposphere from below. Statement-II is scientifically correct.

      
[TRAP ALERT]

        ⚠️ UPSC Trap Alert: This is a cardinal concept repeatedly tested by UPSC (2012, 2019, 2024). The atmosphere is heated predominantly indirectly from below via terrestrial radiation, not directly from above by the Sun.
      
[/TRAP ALERT]

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2024] 
    (Topic: Tropospheric Structure & Convection)

  
  
    Consider the following statements:
    

      * The thickness of the troposphere is greatest at the equator.

      * Strong convection currents transport heat to great heights across the equatorial belt.

    

    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  
  

    * (a) 1 only

    * (b) 2 only

    * (c) Both 1 and 2

    * (d) Neither 1 nor 2

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (c)]

      Detailed Explanation: The troposphere extends up to ~16–18 km at the equator, but only ~8–9 km at the poles. Strong solar insolation at the equator drives intense thermal convection currents that push warm air to great heights, elevating the tropopause. At the poles, dense, cold sinking air compresses the tropospheric column. Statement 2 directly explains Statement 1.

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2019] 
    (Topic: Nocturnal Radiative Cooling & Dew Formation)

  
  
    Why are dewdrops not formed on a cloudy night?
  
  

    * (a) Clouds absorb the radiation released from the Earth's surface.

    * (b) Clouds reflect back the Earth's radiation.

    * (c) The Earth's surface would have low temperature on cloudy nights.

    * (d) Clouds deflect the blowing wind to ground level.

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: Dew formation requires rapid nocturnal radiative cooling so that air in contact with the ground cools below its dew point. On a clear night, longwave terrestrial radiation escapes directly into space (through atmospheric windows). On a cloudy night, clouds act like a blanket: they absorb and re-radiate (reflect/emit back) terrestrial radiation toward the surface, preventing the ground from cooling sufficiently for condensation to occur.

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2018] 
    (Topic: Albedo of Earth Surfaces)

  
  
    Which of the following has the highest albedo?
  
  

    * (a) Black soil

    * (b) Fresh snow

    * (c) Sand desert

    * (d) Prairie grassland

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: Albedo is the fraction of incident solar radiation reflected by a surface. Standard albedo rankings: Fresh snow (0.80–0.90 / 80–90%) > Sand desert (0.35–0.45) > Prairie grassland (0.15–0.25) > Black soil (0.08–0.15). Fresh snow reflects the vast majority of incoming solar shortwave radiation.

    
  

  
    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2013] 
    (Topic: Continentality & Annual Range of Temperature)

  
  
    The annual range of temperature in the interior of the continents is high as compared to coastal areas. What is/are the reason/reasons?
    

      * Thermal contrast between land and water

      * Variation in altitude between continents and oceans

      * Presence of strong winds in the interior

      * Heavy rains in the interior as compared to coasts

    

    Select the correct answer using the code given below:
  
  

    * (a) 1 only

    * (b) 1 and 2 only

    * (c) 2 and 3 only

    * (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

  

  
    
--- Reveal Official Key, Conceptual Explanation & Trap Alert ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Detailed Explanation: Land heats up and cools down much faster than water due to water's higher specific heat capacity, transparency, convective mixing, and high latent heat of evaporation. Consequently, continental interiors experience scorching summers and freezing winters (extreme annual temperature range), while coasts experience maritime moderation. Statement 1 is the sole valid cause. Statements 3 and 4 are factually incorrect (interiors generally receive less rain than coasts).

## 🔮 High-Yield Predictive Prelims MCQs for Upcoming UPSC CSE
-------------------------------------------------------

Curated by Analyzing Emerging Geoscientific Trends, IPCC AR6 & New UPSC Question Formats

  
    [Predictive MCQ 1] 
    (Climatology: Marine Heatwaves (MHWs) & Atmospheric Dynamics)

  
  
    Consider the following statements regarding Marine Heatwaves (MHWs):
    

    Statement-I: Marine heatwaves can occur during any season and can be triggered by persistent atmospheric high-pressure systems (blocking anticyclones).

    Statement-II: Atmospheric blocking ridges suppress cloud formation, maximize incoming solar insolation, and reduce wind-driven ocean mixing, concentrating thermal energy in the upper ocean layer.

    Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?
  
  

    * (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I

    * (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I

    * (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

    * (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Conceptual Framework: Marine Heatwaves (MHWs) are defined when seawater temperatures exceed the 90th percentile of the 30-year climatological baseline for at least five consecutive days. Atmospheric blocking anticyclones create clear skies, maximum insolation, and weak surface winds that choke vertical ocean mixing and upwelling. Statement-II is the direct physical mechanism behind Statement-I.

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 2] 
    (Geomagnetism: South Atlantic Anomaly & Geodynamo)

  
  
    Consider the following statements regarding the Earth's magnetic field:
    

      * The South Atlantic Anomaly (SAA) represents a region where Earth's magnetic field is exceptionally weak, exposing low-orbit satellites to increased radiation hazards.

      * Geomagnetic field reversals occur at strictly uniform, predictable periodic intervals due to the constant speed of outer core convection.

      * Paleomagnetic striping on the ocean floor provides irrefutable evidence of both seafloor spreading and past geomagnetic reversals.

    

    How many of the above statements are correct?
  
  

    * (a) Only one

    * (b) Only two

    * (c) All three

    * (d) None

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b) [Statements 1 and 3 only]]

      Detailed Explanation: Statement 1 is correct: The South Atlantic Anomaly (SAA) is an area stretching from South America to southwest Africa where Earth's magnetic shield is significantly weaker, allowing the inner Van Allen radiation belt to dip closer to Earth's surface. Statement 2 is incorrect: Geodynamo reversals are chaotic, non-periodic, and stochastic (intervals between reversals have ranged from tens of thousands of years to tens of millions of years, e.g., the Cretaceous Superchron). Statement 3 is correct: Symmetrical magnetic polarity stripes on either side of mid-oceanic ridges prove seafloor spreading.

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 3] 
    (Deep Earth Interior: Core-Mantle Boundary & ULVZs)

  
  
    In geophysics, 'Ultra-Low Velocity Zones' (ULVZs) are frequently observed by seismologists at the:
  
  

    * (a) Mohorovičić discontinuity between crust and mantle

    * (b) Core-Mantle Boundary (D'' layer) at ~2,900 km depth

    * (c) Lehmann discontinuity within the Earth's core

    * (d) Lithosphere-Asthenosphere Boundary (LAB)

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: ULVZs are thin patches (5 to 30 km thick) located immediately above the core-mantle boundary (Gutenberg discontinuity / D'' layer) where seismic shear waves (S-waves) drop in velocity by up to 30–50% and P-waves by 10–25%. They represent partially molten mantle material and are widely believed to be the roots of deep mantle plumes.

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 4] 
    (Geomorphology: Peneplain vs Pediplain Evolutionary Models)

  
  
    Consider the following pairs of geomorphic terms and their associated formative processes:
    

      * Peneplain: Fluvial down-wearing and gradual slope reduction approaching base level (W.M. Davis).

      * Pediplain: Parallel slope retreat and pediment coalescence under semi-arid conditions (L.C. King).

      * Etchplain: Deep subsurface chemical weathering followed by stripping of regolith under changing climates.

    

    How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
  
  

    * (a) Only one

    * (b) Only two

    * (c) All three

    * (d) None

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (c) [All three pairs]]

      Detailed Explanation: All three are seminal geomorphic concepts: Davis proposed peneplanation via slope decline; King proposed pediplanation through scarp retreat and pediment extension in arid/semi-arid regions; Wayland and Büdel developed etchplanation, explaining stripped basal surfaces of weathered crystalline shields.

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 5] 
    (Bioclimatology: Wet-Bulb Temperature Threshold)

  
  
    With reference to 'Wet-Bulb Temperature', consider the following statements:
    

      * It represents the lowest temperature to which air can be cooled solely by the evaporation of water into it at constant pressure.

      * A wet-bulb temperature of 35°C marks the theoretical upper physiological limit of human survivability, beyond which metabolic heat dissipation ceases.

      * Unlike dry-bulb temperature, wet-bulb temperature is independent of relative humidity.

    

    Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  
  

    * (a) 1 and 2 only

    * (b) 2 and 3 only

    * (c) 1 only

    * (d) 1, 2 and 3

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (a)]

      Detailed Explanation: Statement 1 is the thermodynamic definition of wet-bulb temperature. Statement 2 is correct: At a wet-bulb temperature of 35°C (95°F), even healthy humans resting in the shade cannot cool down via perspiration; hyperthermia ensues within hours. Statement 3 is false: Wet-bulb temperature is intrinsically dependent on relative humidity (when RH is 100%, wet-bulb temperature equals dry-bulb temperature).

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 6] 
    (Geochemistry & Carbon Cycle: Chemical Silicate Weathering)

  
  
    Which of the following processes acts as Earth's natural long-term geological thermostat by sequestering atmospheric carbon dioxide over millions of years?
  
  

    * (a) Mechanical thermal exfoliation of granitic domes

    * (b) Chemical weathering of silicate rocks by carbonic acid

    * (c) Oxidation of ferromagnesian basaltic minerals

    * (d) Carbonation and dissolution of exposed limestone karst landscapes

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: Under the Urey reaction (CaSiO₃ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + SiO₂), dissolved CO₂ in rainwater (carbonic acid) breaks down silicate minerals on continents. Rivers carry dissolved calcium and bicarbonate ions to oceans, where marine calcifiers lock them into carbonate sediments on the seafloor, drawing down atmospheric CO₂ over geological timescales (millions of years).

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 7] 
    (Atmospheric Physics: The Mesospheric Cooling Paradox)

  
  
    While rising concentrations of greenhouse gases (CO₂ and CH₄) cause warming in the troposphere, what is their observed impact on the mesosphere?
  
  

    * (a) Strong thermal expansion and rapid warming

    * (b) Radiative cooling and atmospheric contraction

    * (c) Complete stabilization of the lapse rate

    * (d) Zero temperature change due to absence of water vapour

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: In the dense troposphere, CO₂ absorbs infrared radiation and transfers heat via molecular collisions, trapping warmth. However, in the rarefied mesosphere, where collisions are rare, excited CO₂ molecules emit infrared radiation directly out into space, acting as an efficient radiative coolant. Rising CO₂ causes the mesosphere to cool and contract.

    
  

  
    [Predictive MCQ 8] 
    (Indian Physical Geography: Bhabar–Terai Piedmont Hydrology)

  
  
    Consider the following statements regarding the Bhabar and Terai belts of the Indo-Gangetic Plain:
    

      * The Bhabar is characterized by porous pebble gravel beds where Himalayan streams infiltrate underground.

      * The Terai zone lies north of the Bhabar, characterized by coarse boulders and dry riverbeds.

      * The spring-line marking the emergence of underground Bhabar streams occurs at the northern margin of the Terai.

    

    Which of the statements given above are correct?
  
  

    * (a) 1 and 2 only

    * (b) 1 and 3 only

    * (c) 2 and 3 only

    * (d) 1, 2 and 3

  

  
    
--- Reveal Explanation & Core Concept ---

    
      
[OFFICIAL KEY: Correct Answer: (b)]

      Detailed Explanation: The Bhabar is a 8–10 km wide piedmont belt of unassorted boulders and pebbles along the Siwalik foothills where streams disappear underground (Statement 1 correct). The Terai lies south of the Bhabar, not north (Statement 2 is false). The boundary between Bhabar and Terai is marked by a prominent spring-line where the subterranean water re-emerges to form marshes and swamps (Statement 3 is correct).
